      __NOTES__
    Metric definitions and full context

    Average FPS = 1000 ÷ mean frame time. 1% low = 1000 ÷ mean of the slowest ceil(1% × N) frame times. P99 is the 99th percentile of frame time. True lows require per-frame capture; 0.1% low needs at least 1,000 samples. Verdicts describe average FPS, not pacing, visibility or input latency. Confidence intervals resample complete paired rounds and are not corrected for multiple comparisons. A cap can lower average FPS while improving pacing.
