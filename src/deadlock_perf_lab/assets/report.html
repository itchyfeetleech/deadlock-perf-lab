Metric definitions & full experiment context

Average FPS = 1000 ÷ mean frame time. 1% low = 1000 ÷ mean of the slowest ceil(1% × N) frame times. P99 is a frame-time percentile, not that slow-tail mean. True lows require per-frame captures; 0.1% low also requires 1,000 samples. These are rendered-frame timings, not input latency. Exploratory intervals are not adjusted for testing multiple treatments.
