How to read these results

Each value is the arithmetic mean of three captures. Percentage differences compare with the mean of three baseline captures. P99 improvement is calculated as 100 × (baseline P99 / tested P99 − 1); positive values mean lower frame time (better). The variance badge marks FPS coefficient of variation ≥ 1%.

The original session and its continuation share the same recorded settings and profiles. Matching configuration hashes do not prove zero performance drift. These are exploratory results from one machine and scene; three captures do not establish a statistically significant improvement. Many CVARs are hidden from console readback, so file application does not prove that the engine used the requested value. Replay camera and visual effects still require review. Lower visual quality can affect gameplay cues.
